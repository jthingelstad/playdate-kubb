Woodcut: three tones, hard shadows, a white pitch

White is live wood. Grey is wood that is down. Black is outline, shadow and anything spent. The pitch stays white; depth comes from grass tufts, line weight and the rough beyond the lines.

Tones and patterns

WhiteThe pitch, standing kubbs, the king, a baton in the air.
BlackOutlines, the side face away from the light, ground shadows, batons at rest, the top rail, cards.
Grey: 50% checkerThe only grey. Fallen kubbs and the drill band, nothing else. Always inside a black outline so it holds in dim light.
Rough: 2 x 1 dashes on an 8 px gridOutside the sidelines and past the far baseline. Part of the cached scenery. Never on the pitch.

Ground cues

Pieces at near, middle and far size (2x)

Type

TOK! ASH WINS BATON 3 OF 6 · LOFT 35° · WIND BACK

Display: a cut wood-type face for cards, bursts and big numbers, drawn as a 1-bit bitmap font at two cap heights, 16 and 32 px. Londrina Solid Black stands in here.

Labels: Roobert 10 Bold from the system, capitals only, white on the black rail or black on a white plaque. Space Grotesk Bold stands in here. Asheville is retired.

Voice

  • During play a label is 12 characters or fewer and starts with a verb or a noun: WIND BACK, FLICK, IN THE BAND, SHORT.
  • Sentences live on cards only, and one per line.
  • Counts are pips before they are numbers.
  • Hits speak wood: TOK, KLAK-KLAK, KING. No POW.
  • Buttons are a ringed A or B glyph followed by one word.

Frame budget notes

  • Cached once per aim view: pitch lines, rough, stakes, aim-view tufts, the rail, every plaque.
  • Rail and plaque text is drawn into its image only when the value changes. No text call in a normal frame.
  • Impact camera tufts: up to 24 pre-drawn tuft images at three sizes, projected each frame. About 24 calls. Check on hardware; drop to 12 if needed.
  • Gauge: one disc image, one arc, one line, one knob: 4 calls.
  • Bursts: pre-drawn images, one call per frame, plus up to 6 chip rectangles.
  • King burst: one full-screen invert for 2 frames. Nothing else is full-screen during play.
  • Speed lines: 3 lines behind the baton while it is in the air.
