HUD layout

A black rail on top, three plaques along the bottom, nothing in the middle. Positions are in screen pixels, x y w h, on the 400 x 240 screen.

1 Rail · 0 0 400 14 · always on. Pennant at x 4, name at x 17, pips centred on x 188 (batons 3 x 8 every 7 px, kubbs 5 x 6 every 10 px), target count right-aligned to x 395. Thrown pips are hollow; the current one is underlined.
2 Prompt flag · 316 158 70 15 · aiming only. Sits over the gauge, 12 characters at most. Inverts to white on black for FLICK.
3 Gauge · disc centre 346 205, radius 27, with a 2 px white halo · aiming only. Bottom right, beside the crank. Wind-up arc at radius 22, 6 px wide. Full-power mark at 10 o'clock. Drill band is a grey wedge from radius 12 to 27 with two black ticks outside the rim.
4 Loft and power · 250 199 62 34 · aiming only. Power bar at 300 205 6 22; the drill band is a grey block across it.
5 Cut flag · 250 182 62 15 · drilling, only while A or B is held.
6 Minimap · frame 4 162 49 76, pitch 45 x 72 at 9 px per metre · always on. Bottom left, so far kubbs never pass under it.
7 Clear area · the full width down to y 156, then 58 16 188 222 below that. The camera keeps the aim line and every live target inside it. Nothing is ever drawn over it except a burst.
8 Result callout · 59 185 234 48 · after a drilled kubb stops. After any release the gauge and loft plaque give way to a power chip at 318 203 76 32 and a result flag at 318 185 76 15.
Gone: the prompt box in the middle of the pitch, the debug readout along the top, the throw label box at the top left. Readout stays in the System Menu.
