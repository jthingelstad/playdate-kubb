Hit effects

Three pre-drawn images per burst, placed up and to the left of the contact point with one long spike touching it. Frame counts are at 30 fps inside the slow motion.

1 Flash · 2 frames 2 Burst · 12 frames, with the sound 3 Break-up · 4 frames
Clean hitOne kubb down. A dry knock.1 image and 6 chips.
DoubleTwo or more kubbs with one baton. The second star lands 3 frames after the first.2 images and 6 chips.
KingThe winning hit, or the losing one. The whole screen inverts for the flash; the burst wears the crown.1 invert, 1 image, 6 chips.

No burst for a baton that touches a kubb and leaves it standing: the kubb's rock is the effect. A miss gets nothing. Sizes on screen: clean 104 x 76, double 150 x 96, king 124 x 92.
